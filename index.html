Privacy Policy

for Colorsort Puzzle a mobile puzzle game

Last updated: 01-10-2026

Short version

  • The app itself does not collect, store or send any personal information.
  • Your game progress stays on your device only, in local storage.
  • The app may display advertisements served by Google AdMob, which uses the standard Android advertising ID and handles its own data processing.
  • You can opt out of personalized ads any time from your device's settings.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Colorsort Puzzle ("the App", "we", "us"), operated by Lakshay Gupta, handles information when you use the App. We are committed to protecting your privacy and keeping things simple and transparent.

By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data collected by the App

The App does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, no login, no email registration, no cloud sync and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK built into the App. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other identifier.

2.2 Data stored locally on your device

To remember your progress, the App stores small pieces of data on your own device (for example, which levels you have completed, your stars and best move counts). This storage is local-only: it never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or to any server. Deleting the App will permanently erase this data.

2.3 Anonymous usage for improvement

We do not currently use any third-party analytics. If we add analytics or other data collection in the future, this policy will be updated and those changes will be reflected below before they take effect.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App may display advertisements in the future, provided by Google AdMob. AdMob is operated by Google LLC and its privacy practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

When the AdMob SDK is active, it may collect and process data such as:

  • the Android Advertising ID (a resettable, non-personal device identifier);
  • IP address and general device information (device model, OS version, screen size, language);
  • information about the app itself and how ads are interacted with;
  • for personalized ads, approximate location and usage data — always in accordance with Google's policies and applicable law.

This data is used to deliver ads, prevent fraud, measure ad performance and, where permitted, show ads that are more relevant to you.

AdMob is described here in advance of ad roll-out. If and when ads go live, this section describes exactly how advertising works. No ad or SDK data is processed by the App before ads are activated.

4. Your Advertising ID & Your Choices

You remain in control of advertising data on your device:

  • Opt out of personalized ads: on your Android device, open Settings > Privacy > Ads and turn on "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization". You may still see ads, but they will be less relevant.
  • Reset your Advertising ID at any time from the same settings screen.
  • You can manage interests and opt out of personalized advertising globally via Google Ads Settings.

5. Consent (GDPR, LGPD, and US State Laws)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or in regions governed by the LGPD (Brazil), CCPA/CPRA, or other privacy laws, we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent framework, if and when ads are enabled, to obtain your freely given permission before personalized advertising or any advertising at all is shown where the law requires it.

You can withdraw or change your consent at any time. If you change consent, the relevant advertising features will be updated accordingly and, where applicable, ads will stop being shown.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in certain regions) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at lakshayanil165@gmail.com and we will take steps to delete it promptly.

If the App is ever designated as aimed at children, we will comply with the applicable children's privacy regulations, including enabling the appropriate AdMob settings for child-directed apps.

7. Data Security

Because the App does not transmit any personal data, there is very little to protect on our side. The local data stored on your device is protected by your device's own operating system security. Where third-party providers (such as Google) process data, they apply industry-standard security and comply with applicable obligations under relevant data-protection laws.

8. Data Retention & Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access & deletion: delete all locally stored App data by uninstalling the App or using your device's "clear app data" option — no account or action on our side is needed.
  • Refuse ads: opt out of personalized advertising as described in Section 4.
  • Withdraw consent: change or revoke consent for advertising as described in Section 5.

Data processed by advertising partners is retained only as long as those partners need it to deliver and measure ads, in line with Google's own data-retention policies.

9. International Data Transfers

Google may process advertising data on servers located outside your country of residence. Where data is transferred outside the EEA/UK, Google relies on recognized safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses to provide an adequate level of protection.

10. Third-Party Services & Links

The App does not contain external links to third-party websites. The only third-party service that may process data is Google AdMob, which processes data in accordance with Google's policies. Where applicable, AdMob's data handling is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change, and — where required — you will be given appropriate notice before the changes take effect. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your privacy, please contact us at lakshayanil165@gmail.com.
